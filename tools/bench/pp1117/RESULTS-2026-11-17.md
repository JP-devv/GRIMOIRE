## Found

- Qwen3.8-27B 8-bit on ONE card: garbage answers in v1.10.7 and rc1 (fixed in bc11f9b); v1.10.5 answered right.

- Flash-Next two cards at 4K reads ~550-665 tok/s on v1.10.5 / v1.10.7 / rc1 alike (RAM experts over PCIe per call);
  the README's 1,474 (10-28) is not reproduced on this Tower today.
- Flash-Next MTP depth 1 / 2 on two cards: no consistent gain (stays off).
- Model suite (regress_all_g0, cache on) on rc1: 23 / 23 identical to v1.10.7.
